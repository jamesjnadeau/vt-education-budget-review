## What it is good for, and what it is not

It is a **summary of enacted law by the Board's own counsel**, and useful as exactly that:
evidence of how the Board understands its charge, and a map of the rulemaking calendar. It is
not authority for what the law says. Every legal proposition in it — the § 11 definitions and
their prospective repeal, the pause on the three-year noncompliance clock, the Rule 2200 parity
requirement — is traceable to 2025 Acts and Resolves No. 73 and 2026 Acts and Resolves No. 170,
and is cited there in the docs page above.

Two slides state things worth pinning to the acts rather than to the deck:

- **"Compliance 'pause'"** — the deck says noncompliance does not start the three-year clock
  until the rules are updated, ending July 1, 2027 at the latest. That is Act 170, Sec. 29a(b),
  and the act's own wording is "whichever date shall come first."
- **"Statutory sunset"** — the deck says the statutory definitions are scheduled for repeal.
  That is Act 170, Sec. 29e(a): repealed on the **July 1 following** the effective date of the
  rules adopted under Sec. 29d, so the repeal date depends on when the Board finishes.
