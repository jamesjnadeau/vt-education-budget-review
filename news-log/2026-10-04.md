- PAINE MOUNTAIN WEIGHS CLOSING BOTH HIGH SCHOOLS AND TUITIONING STUDENTS OUT — and asks whether a unified district can SPLIT under Act 170. From the same DRM update plus The Bridge (below). Superintendent MATTHEW FEDDERS is studying two options: close one middle/high school and consolidate grades 6-12 on one campus (The Bridge reports ~50% community support), or CLOSE BOTH HIGH SCHOOLS, consolidate grades 6-8, and TUITION HIGH SCHOOLERS to neighbors — he has asked about capacity at MONTPELIER HIGH and U-32 for Northfield students and SPAULDING for Williamstown students, and called tuitioning "a lot easier" operationally. He also raised, without an answer, whether Act 170 lets an existing unified district DIVIDE with its towns joining different successor districts. New on this beat: the Sept 7 entry logged Paine Mountain's 6-12 forums; full high-school closure with tuitioning, and the split question, are new. https://www.drm.com/articles/vermont-school-consolidation-progress-update-09-28-2026/

- CORRECTION — THE BAD-FAITH PENALTY IS LOSS OF LEGACY DEBT AID (75% OF DEBT SERVICE ON PRE-2025 PROJECTS), AND THERE IS AN APPEAL — Compass Vermont, "Vermont School Districts Must Join Merger Talks in Good Faith or Risk Paying Their Own School Debt" (Sept 3, 2026), now READ IN FULL after the Sept 27 run logged it as UNREAD (HTTP 429), confirmed against the AOE Education Transformation page. Two corrections to what this log has carried since Sept 13 on DRM's authority: (1) the penalty is ineligibility for LEGACY DEBT AID — state payment of 75% OF ANNUAL DEBT SERVICE on school construction projects APPROVED BEFORE 2025 — not a generic loss of "construction-debt assistance" or of the new 30-75% Act 170 project aid; the bad-faith determination is reported to lawmakers by DEC 1, 2028; (2) the Sept 13 entry repeated DRM's statement that "no due process or appeal mechanism is defined" — the AOE page says a district flagged as a bad-faith participant MAY APPEAL TO THE SECRETARY OF EDUCATION. New figures: the Legislature gave the Vermont Learning Collaborative $442,000 for facilitation, and states an INTENT to spend up to $45.75 MILLION A YEAR on legacy debt aid, APPROPRIATION STILL PENDING — the same authorization-without-money pattern logged Sept 27 for the $50 million new-construction capacity. Open legal question Compass raises: whether one board member's conduct can cost the whole district. Published inside an earlier window; logged now because it was unread then. https://www.compassvermont.com/p/vermont-school-districts-must-join and https://education.vermont.gov/schools/school-governance/education-transformation
