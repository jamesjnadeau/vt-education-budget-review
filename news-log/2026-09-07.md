- Blue Mountain Union teacher contract fight puts the Act 73-era EXCESS SPENDING THRESHOLD on the record as the binding constraint — VTDigger (Sept 4, 2026) reports teachers in Blue Mountain Union School District (about 400 students across three Orange County towns) opened the school year without a contract after the board voted down a July 2026 tentative agreement twice, on July 30 and Aug 5; Vermont-NEA filed a complaint with the Vermont Labor Relations Board on Aug 6 alleging bad-faith bargaining and retaliation. Salary figures are confidential, but board member Dan LaCoss said the proposed contract would push the district over Vermont's excess spending threshold — currently 115.5% of state average per-pupil costs for FY2027 — which triggers additional local taxation. The story states that legislation passed in 2026 GRADUALLY LOWERS that threshold, putting districts under intense pressure to cut costs. This is the first item in this watch where a state per-pupil spending cap is the stated cause of a district-level labor dispute, and it is the operational twin of the class size staffing effects logged in the Aug 25 addenda: the same statutory pressure, showing up in bargaining rather than in staffing counts. Threshold mechanics (115.5% for FY2027, declining) are new to this log. https://vtdigger.org/2026/09/04/tensions-mount-over-teachers-contract-in-blue-mountain-union-school-district/

## Addendum — all three Secretary's Recommended Actions obtained and read

- CORRECTION TO THE MAIN 2026-09-07 ENTRY ABOVE. The user supplied all three Secretary's Recommended Action PDFs directly, closing the ROBOTS_DISALLOWED gap that entry flagged. Having read them, the framing in that entry — that three simultaneous Rule 2200 actions represent "a materially larger public-dollars question" — is WRONG and must not be carried forward. All three are routine five-year renewals, all recommended for APPROVAL, and each document states in terms: "FISCAL IMPLICATIONS: none."

- FAY HONEY KNOPP SCHOOL, Rutland — renewal of THERAPEUTIC independent school approval, grades 7-12 (ages 12-17). Secretary recommends the State Board grant renewal for the full five-year term, through June 30, 2031. Procedural record: Secretary appointed a review committee to visit the school per SBE Rule 2224.4; Head of School provided a programming update and verification of compliance with Rule Series 2200 on March 31, 2025; Annual Compliance Assurance documentation submitted by the Feb 24, 2026 deadline. Statutory authority 16 V.S.A. § 166(b) and State Board Rules 2200 (Rule 2224, Independent School Program Approval). Fiscal implications: none. Staff available: Independent School Program Manager, Special Education Liaison.

- MOUNT ST. JOSEPH ACADEMY, Rutland — renewal of INELIGIBLE independent school approval, grades 9-12; NEASC accredited. Secretary recommends renewal for the full five-year term, through June 30, 2031. Compliance verification provided April 7, 2025; Annual Compliance Assurance deadline of Feb 24, 2026 met per Rule 2223.4. Fiscal implications: none.

- ST. MICHAEL CATHOLIC SCHOOL, Brattleboro — renewal of INELIGIBLE independent school approval, kindergarten through grade twelve; NEASC accredited. Secretary recommends renewal for the full five-year term, through June 30, 2031. Compliance verification provided March 28, 2025; Annual Compliance Assurance deadline of Feb 24, 2026 met per Rule 2223.4. Fiscal implications: none.

- IDENTICAL CONDITION ON ALL THREE, worth recording because it is the only substantive constraint attached: each approval is conditional on the school reporting to the Agency of Education within five business days whenever any change occurs in enrollment, programs, policies, facilities, FINANCIAL CAPACITY, staffing or administration during the approval period. Financial capacity being a named reporting trigger is the one funding-adjacent hook in these documents.

- WHY THE PUBLIC-DOLLARS FRAMING WAS WRONG — the distinction to keep: two of the three (Mount St. Joseph, St. Michael) are approved as INELIGIBLE independent schools, which is precisely the status that does NOT carry eligibility to enroll publicly tuitioned students. Fay Honey Knopp is approved in a different category (therapeutic independent school), and its document likewise states no fiscal implications. The general proposition logged Aug 28 — that Rule Series 2200 approval is the mechanism determining whether an independent school may enroll publicly tuitioned students — remains true as a description of the rule, but it does NOT follow that any particular Rule 2200 agenda item is a funding item.

- METHOD LESSON FOR FUTURE RUNS, the durable takeaway: do not infer funding significance from the regulatory mechanism a document sits under. This watch escalated three routine renewal memos into a headline on the strength of the rule they were filed under, without the documents. When AOE documents are unreadable, log their existence and say the substance is unknown — which the entry did — but do NOT simultaneously assert what they signify. The tell here was available in the document titles: "ineligible independent school approval" is stated on the face of two of the three.
