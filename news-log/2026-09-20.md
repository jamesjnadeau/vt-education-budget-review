- SECRETARY SAUNDERS CAPS NEW SUPERINTENDENT CONTRACTS AT ONE YEAR to keep merged districts from inheriting SEPARATION COSTS — VTDigger carrying an Addison Independent story (Sept 16, 2026). In a Friday email to district leadership, Secretary of Education Zoie Saunders said she would approve new superintendent hires only on ONE-YEAR CONTRACTS, renewable annually, rather than multi-year deals, effective immediately statewide rather than as existing contracts expire. The stated reason is financial and is squarely on this beat: newly formed merged districts would inherit existing superintendent contracts and could be OBLIGATED TO PAY SEPARATION COSTS. Saunders: "I would only approve an interim superintendent under a one-year contract, which could be renewed annually thereafter." Addison Northwest School District is named as affected mid-search. Precedent noted: Secretary Rebecca Holcombe imposed a similar restriction during the Act 46 merger years (2015-2019). This is a new lever in the Act 170 implementation toolkit and the first one this watch has seen applied to district administrative costs. https://vtdigger.org/2026/09/16/state-restricts-new-superintendent-hires-to-one-year/

- THE ACT 170 QUALITY FRAMEWORK, READ IN FULL — merger proposals will be judged partly on FINANCIAL SUSTAINABILITY, via an AOE/State Board rubric and an EQUITY INDEX. The Sept 13 entry logged only that item (H) was on the Sept 16 State Board agenda; the presentation PDF was obtained and read this run. The framework is a joint AOE/State Board approach to "a clear, consistent, and transparent framework for evaluating proposed district mergers," assessing three dimensions: EDUCATIONAL QUALITY (whether a proposed structure provides adequate staffing, programming and resources), EQUITY AND OPPORTUNITY (an EQUITY INDEX used to identify disparities and assess resource allocation), and FINANCIAL SUSTAINABILITY (whether the district can sustain quality within available resources). Deliverables named: rubrics, equity indices, DISTRICT PROFILES, FINANCIAL MODELING TOOLS and documentation templates. HONEST LIMIT, worth recording: the deck sets NO dollar thresholds, NO per-pupil standards, NO class-size numbers, NO accountability benchmarks and NO rulemaking dates — it is criteria architecture, not criteria. That matters because these are the standards the 18 committees must satisfy by Sept 1, 2027.
