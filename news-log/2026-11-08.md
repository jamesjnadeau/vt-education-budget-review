- BARRE VOTERS APPROVE $56.6M SCHOOL BUDGET ON FOURTH ATTEMPT — LAST DISTRICT IN THE STATE TO PASS A FY BUDGET — VTDigger (Nov 4, 2026; read in full) and Vermont Public (Nov 4) / town clerk unofficial results (Nov 3). Barre Unified Union School District passed $56,621,592 with 58% yes overall (Barre Town 55%, Barre City 62%); unofficial tally Yes 1,599, No 1,263, Blank 9. The budget is 1.2% above last year's spending and about half a million dollars below the rejected September version (about $1.2M below it per the clerk-results coverage); earlier rejected plans were $57.8M (Town Meeting Day) and $57.1M (September). Cuts: four vacant staff positions left unfilled, the roughly $100,000 building-maintenance reserve dropped, about $200,000 in technical-center tuition cut; winter sports and after-school activities were spared. While unbudgeted the district took a $6.4M line of credit (up to $22,000/month interest) and could spend only 87% of prior-year spending. Board chair Garrett Grant: "feeling really great about the community support." Tax impact not reported. What changed: resolves the Nov 3 vote carried as an open thread since Oct 6. https://vtdigger.org/2026/11/04/barre-voters-approve-school-district-budget-on-4th-attempt/

- WASHINGTON COUNTY VOTERS APPROVE $54.9M CENTRAL VERMONT CAREER CENTER BOND — VTDigger (Nov 5, 2026; read in full). Bond passed about 60%, 9,753 to 6,609 across the county's 18 towns; a companion 30-year lease from Washington County Unified Union School District passed with 62%. New East Montpelier campus beside U-32 plus the existing Spaulding (Barre) site would raise capacity from ~250 to ~450 students (400+ applied last year, per director Jody Emerson). Construction as soon as spring 2028, opening fall 2030. Follows voters' November 2025 rejection of a $149M standalone Barre bond. State construction aid and tax impact not covered in the article. https://vtdigger.org/2026/11/05/washington-county-voters-approved-54-9-million-bond-for-new-career-center/
