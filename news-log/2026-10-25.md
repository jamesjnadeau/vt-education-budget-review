# Vermont Education Funding Watch — 2026-10-25

- TAMMY KOLBE ELECTED STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION CHAIR; BOARD FACES JANUARY GRADUATION-REQUIREMENTS AND MERGER-EVALUATION DEADLINES — VTDigger, "National education funding expert takes the helm of the State Board of Education" (Oct 22, 2026). At its Oct 21 meeting the board unanimously elected Kolbe, an education finance researcher and former UVM professor who had been acting chair, to finish the term of Jennifer Samuelson (who resigned abruptly mid-meeting in September citing personal reasons) through the end of February; Cynthia Stuart (consultant, fiscal regulation background) is vice chair. A board panel will draft the framework for evaluating school-merger proposals "in the coming months," and statewide high school graduation requirements are due by January. House Ways and Means Chair Emilie Kornheiser praised Kolbe as data-driven. No specific funding actions reported. Resolves the Oct 21 meeting thread logged Oct 11/18 (held; Kolbe was the acting chair named in the Oct 18 Title I item). https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/22/national-education-funding-expert-takes-the-helm-of-the-state-board-of-education/

- GAP-FILL: VERMONT SPENDS ~$2.5B A YEAR ON 73,000 STUDENTS; NON-EDUCATION TAXES DIVERTED TO HOLD DOWN PROPERTY TAX — WCAX, "Vermont's $2.5 billion school costs squeeze funding for other programs" (Oct 7, 2026, Calvin Cutler; published in an earlier window and missed, logged now). About $1B more than 20 years ago despite ~15,000 fewer students. To limit property tax increases the Legislature has diverted the entire sales and use tax, purchase and use tax, one-quarter of meals and rooms, and all lottery proceeds — $2.3B over three years — plus $200M shifted from other General Fund taxes; another ~$500M of non-education revenue is forecast over the next three years to hold the average increase to 5%. Tax Commissioner Bill Shouldice: "It's got to stop. The time is now." A Vermont Public poll (Sept 28) found 41% say keeping property taxes low matters more than keeping schools in their communities. https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/07/vermonts-25-billion-school-costs-squeeze-funding-other-programs/
