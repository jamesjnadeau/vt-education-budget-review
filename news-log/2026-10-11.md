- BARRE BOARD SETS A $56.6M, +1.2% BUDGET FOR A FOURTH VOTE ON NOV. 3 — WCAX (Oct 6, 2026). Resolves the open thread carried since Sept 20/27/Oct 4: at its Oct 5 meeting the Barre Unified Union School District board approved a revised budget of $56.6 million, a 1.2% increase over last year, the smallest ask of the year (earlier failed proposals ran from +3.3% down to +2.1%). Cuts: consolidating Barre City and Town administrative duties and temporarily removing some unfilled positions; the board says no student activities, athletics or transportation are affected. The vote falls on the NOV. 3 general election, which was the date the clerks preferred (Oct 20 and Oct 27 were the alternatives logged Sept 27). The article gives no tax-rate impact. https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/06/barre-school-board-approves-revised-budget-fourth-vote/

- RECORD DIESEL PRICES ARE BLOWING HOLES IN SCHOOL TRANSPORTATION BUDGETS — WCAX (Oct 2, 2026) and VTDigger, "Vermont school districts scramble to cover the increased cost of diesel" (Oct 8, 2026; headline only, article UNREAD). WCAX: AAA put Vermont diesel at $6.40 a gallon; Champlain Valley School District expects to spend about $200,000 more than budgeted ($16,000-$20,000 a month); South Burlington did not budget for the increase (buses run ~300,000 miles a year at 5-7 mpg) and may cancel trips and ask parents to drive students to athletic events. New on this beat: an unbudgeted mid-year cost shock that will feed into FY28 budget building and the Education Fund spending picture. Standing caveat: no statewide total found. https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/02/record-diesel-prices-strain-vermont-school-district-budgets/ and https://vtdigger.org/education/
