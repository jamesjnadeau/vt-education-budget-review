- CHITTENDEN COUNTY FAMILY SUES OVER ACT 73 PUBLIC-TUITION LIMITS — VTDigger (Nov 12, 2026; read in full). Carrie and Greg Bishop (Barstow Unified Union School District), represented by Jacob Reed of Alliance Defending Freedom, filed a federal suit against the Agency of Education, Secretary Zoie Saunders, the State Board of Education and Barstow UUSD (AOE represented by the Attorney General's Office), alleging Act 73's tuition rules create a "de facto religious exclusion" violating the First Amendment. Under Act 73 a private school qualifies for public tuition only if its district lacks a public school for some or all grades and at least 25% of its 2023-24 students were funded by a Vermont district; only 18 private schools now qualify, none religious (the law removed 18 secular and 15 Catholic/Christian schools; ~3,500 students received public tuition as of 2024). They seek district-paid tuition for a senior daughter at Mount St. Joseph Academy (Rutland) and dual-enrollment access. AOE spokesperson Toren Ballard declined comment; House Education Chair Peter Conlon (D-Cornwall) says the goal is keeping students in public schools to lower per-pupil costs. Borderline scope (tuition funding eligibility under Act 73).
