- FEDS DESIGNATE VERMONT "HIGH RISK" ON ITS $41M TITLE I, PART A GRANT — VTDigger, "Feds issue warning on grant funds after Vermont identifies own mistakes" (Oct 14, 2026). New federal-funding item with a specific Vermont impact. Vermont discovered in Feb 2026 that its methodology for identifying underperforming schools for support under ESSA (in use since 2017-18) contained errors, reported them to the U.S. Department of Education within hours, and sent a remediation plan in July. In September the department (letter from Assistant Secretary Kirsten Baesler, OESE) designated the state "high risk" on the Title I grant and demanded immediate fixes; Vermont also carried an earlier, separate high-risk designation from 2024. Money at stake: failing the federal deadlines could cost $100,000 in grant funding plus added administrative burdens. Vermont proposed finishing the corrected plan by fall 2028, which Baesler called too long. Deadlines: DEC 15, 2026 for a new identification methodology; January 2027 to apply it to 2025-26. Secretary Zoie Saunders attributes the errors to a burdensome process predating her tenure; Gov. Phil Scott said Vermont should be able to meet its deadlines before being labeled high risk; the State Board of Education (acting chair Tammy Kolbe) unanimously approved a letter in early October asking the feds to reconsider. https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/14/feds-issue-warning-on-grant-funds-after-vermont-identifies-own-mistakes/
