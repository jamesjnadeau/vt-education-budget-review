Covers everything published 2026-10-25 through 2026-11-01.

- OPEN-MEETING-LAW GAP STALLS ACT 170 MERGER STUDY COMMITTEES IN FIVE REGIONS — VTDigger, "Blunder over legal process impedes school merger studies in some regions" (Oct 27, 2026, Charlotte Oliver; read in full). Act 170 does not say who must warn (notice) committee meetings or post agendas, and facilitators and districts each deny the duty. Meetings in five regions have been canceled or postponed; they will not reschedule until legislators or the Secretary of State give an interpretation. Group 17 (St. Albans area; facilitator John Everitt) had its Sept 29 first meeting converted to an information session because members doubted it was properly warned. Lead Facilitator Dave Younce: "If it's end of November and we're still struggling to get clarity, I think it does put our timelines into risk"; he expected guidance "within the next week." The Vermont Learning Collaborative has legal advice it may not need to warn meetings and will not build a statewide website. Superintendents Association director Chelsea Myers wants a central repository ("no one wants to own that piece of it"). AOE spokesperson Toren Ballard: the Agency "cannot satisfy another legal entity's obligations under the Open Meeting Law." Secretary of State chief of staff Bryan Mills: no guidance. Senate Education Chair Seth Bongartz (D-Bennington) says follow the Act 46 model (superintendent of the largest district warns the first meeting). What changed: this answers the open question carried since Oct 15 about whether first meetings actually occurred — in several regions they have not. Final reports remain due Sept 2027; votes Town Meeting Day 2028. https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/27/blunder-over-legal-process-impedes-school-merger-studies-in-some-regions/

- FBI PROBES FORMER BURLINGTON SCHOOL ADMINISTRATOR OVER ALLEGED $1.8M DISTRICT FRAUD — VTDigger (Oct 28, updated Oct 29, 2026, Charlotte Oliver; read in full; first reported by Vermont News First). Affidavit by FBI Special Agent Eric Boyce alleges Stephanie Phillips (Burlington School District 1988-May 2026, most recently director of teaching and learning) used a sham company, Leading and Learning Consultants of VT, LLC, to be paid twice for her regular work and to bill for work never done: at least $1,769,000 since March 2013. Possible wire fraud, mail fraud and theft from programs receiving federal funds; no charges. Search of her Colchester home Oct 16. Company manager listed is Don Collins, her father and a former Senate Education chair. Superintendent Tom Flanagan says the district found discrepancies after she left, reported them (June 2026 police complaint) and will seek to recover funds; the money was earmarked for professional learning. Borderline scope (district finances / federal-program funds, not state funding policy) — logged for the Burlington finance context. https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/28/fbi-investigates-former-burlington-school-administrator-in-alleged-1-8-million-fraud/
